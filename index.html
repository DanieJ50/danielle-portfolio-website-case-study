02 / FEATURED CASE STUDIES

DESIGN THAT STARTS WITH A WHY.

CASE 01

CAPSTONE • UX/UI • BRANDING

SAVESIP

A gamified drink-spending tracker that helps young adults understand how small café purchases add up — without making financial wellness feel cold or spreadsheet-heavy.

THE PROBLEM

Small coffee, boba, and café purchases feel harmless in the moment, but repeat purchases can quietly grow.

THE SHIFT

The concept evolved from the money-forward Cup$i into a warmer habit-building system with mascots, rewards, and savings feedback.

THE SYSTEM

Tap-to-track logging, home-brew comparisons, sip moods, unlockables, challenges, a savings dashboard, and a social recipe layer.

THE PROOF

Audience statements, personas, low-fidelity planning, high-fidelity screens, brand identity, and feature logic.

CASE 02

UX/UI • PERSONALIZATION • WEB

COZY RECIPE BUILDER

A playful recipe-building concept that turns cravings into personalized recipes by starting with flavor, texture, ingredients, and cooking method instead of endless scrolling.
